      How to examine the eyes (OSCE order)
      1. Inspect from the end of the bed, then up close: ptosis, head tilt, pupil size and shape, any squint.
      2. Acuity, each eye, with glasses and pinhole.
      3. Fields by confrontation, one eye at a time, all four quadrants. Red pin for central vision.
      4. Pupils: size in light and dim, direct and consensual reflex, swinging torch, then near.
      5. Eye movements in an H, asking about double vision at each corner. Watch for nystagmus.
      6. Cover test while the patient fixes on your pen.
      7. Offer fundoscopy to finish, then present: describe, localise, give a cause.

      A schematic teaching model. The eye movements come from a simplified muscle model and the pupils from a simplified reflex model, so real patients will vary. Not for clinical decisions.
